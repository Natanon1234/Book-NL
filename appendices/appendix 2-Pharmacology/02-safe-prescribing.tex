\chapter{Safe and Rational Prescribing}
\margintoc

\section{Selecting a treatment}

Rational treatment begins with a clear therapeutic goal and a review of the
patient's relevant conditions, current medicines, allergies, and preferences.
The expected benefit should be weighed against potential harm and practical
constraints.

\section{Medication safety}

Safe medication use depends on accurate prescribing, dispensing, administration,
and follow-up. Clear instructions, medication reconciliation, and attention to
interactions and duplicate therapies can reduce preventable errors.

\section{Monitoring response}

Follow-up should assess whether the intended effect is achieved and whether
adverse effects have occurred. The choice and timing of monitoring depend on
the medicine, the patient's risk factors, and the treatment goal.
