\chapter*{Preface}
Alas, dear reader, medicine is as much a science as it is an art. No one person can claim to know the entire body of knowledge involved in medical practice. However, some fundamentals remain hard truths in the medical field, and they are tested in practically all medical examinations. Therefore, we, as the authors, have aimed to write extensively and, hopefully, in an approachable manner, about the topics that will appear time and time again. We have designed this text around the content outlines provided by the แพทยสภา and have organized it using a systems-based approach to medicine that can serve practitioners at all levels. While no text can claim to be a complete representation of medicine, we aim to represent the current state of the art as accurately as possible. Although we have made every effort to check and verify the knowledge presented in this text, errors may remain. If so, please feel free to contact us. We hope that this text will serve useful for both the medical student looking to prepare for examinations, as well as the practicing medical practitioner. Finally, we hope that you will enjoy reading the text as much as we have enjoyed writing it!

\begin{flushright}
Natanon Kititmongkolsuk\\
Chief Editor
\end{flushright}
